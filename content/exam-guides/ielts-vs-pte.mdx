Both IELTS and PTE Academic are accepted for Australian, UK, Canadian, and New Zealand visas and university admissions. But they're very different exams. Here's everything you need to know to pick the right one.

## The Core Difference

**IELTS** is a human-graded exam. A certified examiner grades your Speaking and Writing. Results take 3–13 days.

**PTE Academic** is fully computer-graded. An AI scores every section, including Speaking. Results are usually available within 48 hours.

## Format Comparison

### IELTS Academic
| Section | Time | Format |
|---------|------|--------|
| Listening | 30 min | Audio recordings, 40 questions |
| Reading | 60 min | 3 passages, 40 questions |
| Writing | 60 min | Task 1 (graph/chart) + Task 2 (essay) |
| Speaking | 11–14 min | Face-to-face with examiner |

### PTE Academic
| Section | Time | Format |
|---------|------|--------|
| Speaking & Writing | 77–93 min | Read aloud, essays, summarize text |
| Reading | 32–41 min | Fill blanks, multiple choice |
| Listening | 45–57 min | Summarize, highlight, dictation |

## Scoring

**IELTS** uses a band score from 1–9 in 0.5 increments. Each section is scored separately. Most visas require an overall band of 6.0–7.0.

**PTE** scores from 10–90. Each section gets a score, and many integrating communicative skills are scored across sections simultaneously. Most Australian visas require 65+.

## Which Is Easier?

It depends on your strengths:

**Choose IELTS if you:**
- Are a strong writer and can express ideas well on paper
- Prefer talking to a real person in the Speaking section
- Are not comfortable with computers
- Want examiner flexibility (human graders can understand context and accents)

**Choose PTE if you:**
- Type faster than you write by hand
- Want results within 48 hours
- Are good at reading aloud with clear pronunciation
- Prefer a fully standardized, unbiased scoring system
- Are Filipino — Filipino accent scores well with PTE's AI

## Cost Comparison (Philippines)

| Exam | Approximate Cost |
|------|-----------------|
| IELTS Academic | ₱14,000–₱16,000 |
| PTE Academic | ₱10,000–₱13,000 |

PTE is generally cheaper in the Philippines.

## Acceptance

Both are widely accepted. For specific use cases:

- **Australia PR (skilled migration)**: Both accepted. PTE is often preferred because of faster results.
- **UK visa**: Both accepted by UKVI. Make sure to book the UKVI version of IELTS specifically.
- **Canada PR**: Both accepted. IELTS is more common for Canada.
- **Philippine nursing board (CGFNS/PearsonVue pathway)**: Check the specific body — requirements vary.

## Retakes

- **IELTS**: You can retake anytime, but you pay full price each time. Some countries/institutions accept individual section scores (IELTS One Skill Retake).
- **PTE**: You can retake anytime. No limit. Results don't expire for 2 years.

## Our Recommendation

If you're applying for **Australian PR or skilled migration**, go with **PTE Academic** — faster results, cheaper in the Philippines, and the AI scoring is consistent.

If you're applying for **UK or Canada**, or if you prefer a human examiner for Speaking, go with **IELTS**.
