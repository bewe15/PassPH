Most test-takers focus on exam strategies — and strategies matter. But if your underlying English level isn't improving, strategies alone will only take you so far. Here's how to build real English skills between practice sessions, using free resources available to anyone in the Philippines.

## The Honest Truth About Band Score Improvement

If you're at Band 5.5 and need Band 7.0, that's not just about exam technique. That's a genuine English proficiency gap that requires consistent daily input over weeks or months. Strategies help you maximize the score at your current level. **Consistent exposure to English raises your level.**

The good news: raising your level doesn't require expensive courses. It requires consistency.

## 1. Read English Every Day (30 Minutes Minimum)

Reading is the single most proven method for improving vocabulary, grammar intuition, and reading speed simultaneously.

**What to read:**
- **BBC News** (bbc.com/news) — formal, clear, varied topics
- **The Guardian** (theguardian.com) — academic tone, opinion articles good for IELTS Task 2 ideas
- **Scientific American** (scientificamerican.com) — good for PTE's academic register
- **Wikipedia** — any topic you're interested in, academic style

**How to read:**
Don't look up every word. First, read for meaning. If a word blocks understanding, look it up. If you understand without it, keep going. Vocabulary that you encounter in context sticks better than flashcard lists.

Aim for **30 minutes of reading daily**. That's roughly 2–3 news articles or one longer piece.

## 2. Listen to English Every Day

For IELTS Listening and PTE Listening, your ear needs to be trained to process English at natural speed with various accents.

**Free listening resources:**

| Resource | Accent | Good For |
|----------|--------|----------|
| BBC World Service | British | IELTS Listening Parts 3 & 4 |
| ABC Australia (Podcasts) | Australian | IELTS all parts |
| TED Talks | International | PTE Listening, academic topics |
| BBC 6 Minute English | British | Short, transcripts available |
| CNN News | American | General comprehension |

**The most effective habit:** Listen to something **while reading the transcript**. BBC 6 Minute English provides full transcripts. This trains your ear to connect sounds to words you already know.

## 3. Write in English Daily (Even Just a Paragraph)

Writing daily improves:
- Grammar accuracy (you catch errors when you re-read)
- Sentence variety (you naturally try different structures)
- Vocabulary recall (using a word in writing cements it)

**What to write:**
- A response to a news article you read that day (agree/disagree)
- A short summary of a YouTube video
- An IELTS Task 2-style opinion paragraph on any topic

You don't need anyone to grade it. The act of writing forces your brain to produce English, which is different from consuming it.

## 4. Build Vocabulary the Smart Way

Don't memorize word lists. Instead, collect words in context.

**The method:**
1. When you read and find an unknown word, write it down with the full sentence
2. Note what part of speech it is (noun, verb, adjective)
3. Write your own sentence using it
4. Review your list once a week

Target **10 new words per week**, used actively in your own writing. That's 520 words a year — enough to noticeably improve your Lexical Resource score.

**High-value vocabulary categories for IELTS/PTE:**
- Academic verbs: *indicate, demonstrate, suggest, contribute, attribute, constitute*
- Discourse markers: *Furthermore, Nevertheless, In contrast, Consequently, Despite this*
- Hedging language: *It could be argued that, There is evidence to suggest, Arguably*

## 5. Speak English Out Loud Daily

Speaking is the skill most Filipino learners underuse in self-study, because it feels uncomfortable without a partner.

**Solo speaking practice methods:**
- **Read aloud** — any text, any time. This directly trains the PTE Read Aloud task.
- **Describe what you're doing** (in English) while cooking, commuting, cleaning
- **Shadow a speaker** — play a YouTube video, pause after each sentence, repeat it immediately trying to match the rhythm and intonation
- **Record yourself** — speak on any topic for 60 seconds, play it back, note hesitations

For IELTS Speaking Part 2 (the long turn), practice speaking for exactly 2 minutes on random topics. Set a timer and don't stop.

## 6. Use Your Phone Commute

Most people spend 30–90 minutes daily commuting in Metro Manila or provincial cities. That's study time.

**What to do on the commute:**
- Listen to BBC or TED Talks (even without looking at the screen)
- Review your vocabulary notebook
- Read one news article
- Listen and shadow a YouTube English lesson

The commute alone gives you 5–10 hours of study time per week without changing anything else about your schedule.

## How Long Does It Take?

| Starting Band | Target Band | Realistic Timeframe |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 5.0 | 6.5 | 4–6 months |
| 6.0 | 7.0 | 2–4 months |
| 6.5 | 7.5 | 3–5 months |
| 7.0 | 8.0 | 6–12 months |

These are realistic averages with **daily** study of 1–2 hours. Inconsistent study takes much longer.
