PTE Academic Reading is different from IELTS Reading in one critical way: **time is shared across all tasks in the section**, and there is no per-question timer. Most test-takers underestimate how fast they need to move. Here's how to stay ahead.

## 1. Know the Question Types Before You Sit the Exam

PTE Reading has 5 question types. Each requires a different approach:

| Question Type | What It Tests | Time to Spend |
|---------------|--------------|---------------|
| Fill in the Blanks (Reading) | Grammar + vocabulary in context | ~1 min per blank |
| Multiple Choice (Single) | Detail comprehension | ~2 min |
| Multiple Choice (Multiple) | Detail comprehension | ~2–3 min |
| Re-order Paragraphs | Text structure and logic | ~2–3 min |
| Fill in the Blanks (Reading & Writing) | Grammar + vocabulary | ~1 min per blank |

Going in blind is the fastest way to waste time. Know what each task looks like so you can start immediately.

## 2. Re-order Paragraphs: Find the Topic Sentence First

Re-order Paragraphs is the task most people find hardest. The trick:

1. **Find the opening sentence** — it introduces the topic without referring to anything before it. It won't start with "However", "This", "They", or "These."
2. **Find the closing sentence** — it often summarizes, concludes, or refers back to the opening idea.
3. **Chain the middle paragraphs** — look for pronouns and connectors that link to the previous sentence.

Practice this as a standalone skill. It significantly boosts your score.

## 3. Fill in the Blanks: Read the Whole Passage First

For both Fill in the Blank types, read the entire passage before filling any blanks. This gives you:
- Context for each gap
- An understanding of the tone and register (formal/informal)
- Clues from surrounding sentences

Then eliminate wrong options by checking grammar. Does the word fit grammatically? Does it match the verb tense? Does it collocate with the words around it?

## 4. Multiple Choice Multiple Answer: Expect to Lose Marks for Wrong Answers

PTE **deducts marks** for wrong answers in Multiple Choice Multiple Answer. This is unlike most other exam formats. The rule:

- Only select options you are **confident** about
- If you're unsure between two options, only select the one you're more confident about
- Leaving a checkbox unchecked is safer than a wrong guess

This is the most penalty-heavy task in PTE Reading. Be conservative.

## 5. Manage Your Total Section Time

The Reading section is 32–41 minutes for the full exam. Roughly allocate:

- **Fill in the Blanks (R):** 6–8 minutes
- **Multiple Choice (single):** 4–5 minutes
- **Multiple Choice (multiple):** 5–6 minutes
- **Re-order Paragraphs:** 8–10 minutes
- **Fill in the Blanks (R&W):** 8–10 minutes

If you're running over time on Re-order Paragraphs, make your best guess and move on. One unfinished task can cascade into lost marks on all remaining tasks.

---

## The Skill That Ties It All Together

All PTE Reading tasks test the same underlying skill: **reading for meaning in context**, not word-for-word translation. If you're translating in your head, you're too slow. Practice reading English texts every day — news articles, opinion pieces, academic summaries — and train yourself to understand paragraphs as units, not sentences.
